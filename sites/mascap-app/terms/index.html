Privacy PolicyTerms of Use

1. Agreeing to these terms

These terms are for every member of staff who uses the MASCAP Hospitals system. You agree to them the first time you sign in. If you do not agree, do not use the system, and tell the hospital's admin.

The system belongs to the hospital's work. StellaXafe Technologies Limited built it and looks after it for the hospital.

2. Your account is yours alone

  • Only staff the hospital has invited may use the system.
  • Sign in with your own work email and your own PIN. Never share your PIN, and never use somebody else's account. Everything done under your PIN carries your name.
  • Lock the screen when you leave the device, and sign out at the end of your shift, so the next person signs in as themselves.
  • If you think somebody knows your PIN, tell the admin straight away.

3. Patient information is confidential

  • Look at a patient's information only when your work for that patient needs it.
  • Never copy, photograph, screenshot, print or send patient information outside the system (for example on WhatsApp or social media), unless the patient's care requires it and the hospital allows it.
  • Never talk about a patient where others can hear, or with people not caring for them.
  • This duty continues after you leave the hospital.

4. Keep records true

  • Record only what really happened, at the time it happened, under your own name.
  • Never delete or hide a mistake. Correct it in the way the system allows, so the record shows what was changed and by whom.

5. Everything is logged

The system writes down who signed in, who opened which record and who changed what. The hospital may review this log at any time, to protect patients.

6. Your devices

  • Keep a screen lock on any phone or computer you use for the system.
  • Do not use the system on a device other people can open without you.
  • If a device with the system on it is lost or stolen, tell the admin at once.

7. Report problems at once

If you see patient information where it should not be, or think somebody has seen what they should not, tell the admin the same day. The hospital must report some of these events to the Nigeria Data Protection Commission within 72 hours.

8. Misuse

Breaking these terms can lead to your account being switched off and to the hospital's own disciplinary steps. Misusing personal information can also be an offence under the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023.

9. Availability

The system can be unavailable at times, for example when the internet is down. When it is, follow the hospital's paper process and enter the records once it is back.

10. Privacy

How the system keeps and protects information is explained in the Privacy Policy.

11. Changes to these terms

When these terms change, the date at the top changes, and you may be asked to agree again.
